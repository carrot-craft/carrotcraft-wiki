<Callout type="info" title="What are special items?">
  Items that play a different **effect and move** for each item when used. There are summer festival items and Halloween decorations. **They are not used up**, and you can use them again once the wait is over.
</Callout>

## 🥕 In short

  <Card title="2. Use it to see the effect" description="Eat a food, or right-click / wear the other items." />
  <Card title="3. Use it again after the wait" description="While the item in your hotbar has a white overlay, you cannot use it." />
</Cards>

<Callout type="warn" title="Where moves do not happen">
  Inside a town where you cannot build, or in a protected area, **only the effect** plays and no move happens. Moves on mobs and animals also do not happen when they are in such a place.
</Callout>

- The wait is per item and per **player**. Switching items does not skip it, and it stays when you log out
- Moves only affect you, hostile mobs and certain animals. They never affect other players, tamed animals, named mobs, villagers or bosses. The shaved ice snowballs do not hit players either
- Eating a food does not restore hunger
- They also work on the resource server
- Special items cannot be put in a composter

## 🛒 Special item shop

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Open `/market`**

    Press "Choose a shop" on the market screen and select "Special Items".
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Click this week's item**

    Check the price on the confirmation screen and buy it. Spent CC is not refunded.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Rotation | Every **Saturday 18:00 (JST)**, **one** item is picked at random from the items above |
| Price | ★5 200,000 CC · ★4 100,000 CC · ★3 50,000 CC · ★2 30,000 CC · ★1 10,000 CC |
| Limit | **One** per player per week (other accounts of the same person count together) |
| Spent CC | Goes nowhere and disappears |

***

## 🎟️ From vote crates

The vote crate and the VoteParty crate can give a **Special Item Crate Key**. Right-click with the key to open it and you **always get one** special item.

## ❓ FAQ

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Nothing happens when I use it">
    You are still waiting. While the item in your hotbar has a white overlay, you cannot use it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The effect plays, but I don't float and mobs don't move">
    You are inside a town where you cannot build, or in a protected area. Only the effect plays there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='It says "You already bought this item this week"'>
    The same item is limited to one per player per week, including purchases on your other accounts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The item I want is not in the shop">
    The shop lists only one item a week. Buy from a player who has it, try for a Special Item Crate Key from vote crates, or wait for next week.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I sell special items at the market?">
    They cannot be sold at the market or the buyback shop.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
