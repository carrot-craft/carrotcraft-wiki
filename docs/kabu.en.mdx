## 🛒 Buying and selling

`/kabu` opens the list of listed nations. Pick a nation, choose how many shares, check the quote, then confirm.

- **You can always buy and always sell.** You don't need to wait for another player (each nation has a trading "pool" you trade against)
- **The more you buy, the higher the price per share; the more you sell, the lower.** The amount shown is an estimate — the final amount is set when the trade goes through
- The fee is **1.5%** of the trade
- `/kabu p` shows your shares and `/kabu h` your trade history

<Callout type="warn">
  Share prices can fall. If a nation empties out and nobody wants its shares, the price drops. **Losses are not compensated.** Only use CC you can afford to lose.
</Callout>

## 💰 Dividends

Every **Saturday at 12:00**, part of the nation's weekly profit (**tax income − upkeep**) is paid to shareholders in proportion to their shares.

- The king sets the payout ratio (dividend rate) from **0 to 100%**
- If the treasury would drop below three weeks of upkeep, that week's dividend is skipped (so the nation doesn't collapse)
- Dividends arrive even while you're offline; you get a message when you're in game

## 🏛️ Listing a nation (for kings)

<Steps>
  <Step>**Apply** — the king applies with `/kabu apply` (listing fee **50,000 CC**)</Step>
  <Step>**Staff review** — staff check the requirements and approve</Step>
  <Step>**Start the offering** — the king sets the total shares, the share to release (10–60%) and the price per share with `/kabu ipo`. Anyone can subscribe for 72 hours</Step>
  <Step>**Listed** — if subscriptions reach half of the shares on offer, the nation is listed and trading begins. Otherwise the offering is cancelled and all subscriptions are refunded</Step>
</Steps>

**Requirements**

| Requirement | Details |
|------|------|
| Nation age | 30 days or more |
| Active residents | 10 or more residents who logged in within the last 30 days |
| King's tenure | 14 days or more |
| Punishments | The king has no punishments in the last 30 days |
| Listing slots | Up to 5 nations in total (staff may change this) |

About 60% of the CC raised in the offering goes to the treasury and 40% to the trading pool. The treasury's share never has to be paid back and can be spent on growing the nation.

## 👑 Rules for kings

The king holds the most shares and knows the nation best. To protect shareholders, kings follow these rules:

- **Keep at least 30% of the shares**
- **Announce any sale of your own shares at least 3 days in advance** (the announcement is public and expires after 7 days)
- CC the king earns from selling shares is **frozen for 30 days**
- The king cannot trade for **24 hours** before and after changing the dividend rate
- The same rules continue to apply to a former king for **90 days** after stepping down

## 🗳️ Shareholder meeting

`/kabu soukai <nation>` opens that nation's shareholder meeting screen.

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Share issues (adding shares to raise CC for the treasury)">
    The king can issue new shares to put CC into the treasury (up to 30% of total shares per year).
    Because there are more shares, each share is worth less and the price drops.

    - The king proposes with `/kabu issue <shares> <reason>`, and shareholders can vote for **48 hours** (`/kabu vote <nation> yes|no`)
    - **Rejected if "against" is more than half of the votes cast.** No votes or a tie means it passes
    - Votes are weighted by the shares held at the deadline. The king and former kings (90 days) cannot vote
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="TOB (takeover bid)">
    An offer to **buy every shareholder's shares** (except the king's) at **at least 1.5× the current price**.

    - A member of the nation (a resident of the capital, or the mayor of a town in the nation) makes it with `/kabu tob <nation> <percent>`. The money is held up front
    - **When the king accepts**, all shares are bought and **the bidder becomes the new king**
    - If the king declines, the bidder withdraws, or 72 hours pass, the held money is refunded in full
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Major shareholder perks">
    | Holding | Perk |
    |-------|------|
    | 1% or more | See the nation's finances in detail (weekly tax income and upkeep) |
    | 5% or more | Listed by name as a major shareholder |
    | The largest holder with 10% or more | Listed by name as the top shareholder |
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

## ⛔ Delisting

A nation is delisted when, for example:

- Its residents fall below 5 and don't recover within 14 days of the warning
- Upkeep stays unpaid (a town goes bankrupt) for 21 days and isn't resolved within 7 days of the warning
- The king requests it (after 30 days), the nation disbands, or staff decide to

On delisting, **the CC in the trading pool is shared out among shareholders in proportion to their shares** (the king and former kings don't receive any). A warning is shown before delisting.

## 🌐 Trading on the website

The **Stocks** page on the official website shows price charts and recent trades. **Log in with Discord** to trade from the website too.

- When you trade on the website, you also get a message in game. If it wasn't you, stop web trading with `/kabu webstop`
- Web trading is available to players who first joined at least 7 days ago and have played for 10 hours or more

## ⌨️ Commands

| Command | Description |
|---------|------|
| `/kabu` | List of listed nations and trading |
| `/kabu p` | Your shares |
| `/kabu h` | Your trade history |
| `/kabu soukai <nation>` | Shareholder meeting (share issues, TOB, major shareholders) |
| `/kabu vote <nation> yes\|no` | Vote for / against a share issue |
| `/kabu tob <nation> <percent>` | Make a TOB (e.g. 150) |
| `/kabu king` | King's screen (application, offering, dividend rate, sale announcements) |
| `/kabu webstop` / `/kabu webstart` | Stop / resume trading from the website |
