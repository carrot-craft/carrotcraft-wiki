Players are split into up to 4 teams. Defend your team's **Nexus** while destroying the Nexuses of the other teams. The last team with a Nexus standing wins. Gather resources, build up your gear, and use your class abilities to share the work of attacking and defending.

## Game Flow

<Steps>
<Step>
### Joining and Voting

Choose "Join a game" in the lobby to enter the queue for an official game. Once 4 players are in, a 2-minute countdown starts and the voting screen opens. You vote on these 2 things:

- PvP (old = 1.8-style spam clicking / new = current combat)
- Skipping the wait (once more than half of the waiting players vote, 10 seconds remain)
</Step>
<Step>
### Team Assignment

With 12 or more players, the game is played with 4 teams. With fewer, it is played with 2 teams. Parties of up to 3 players are placed on the same team. Everyone else is assigned so that the teams are balanced.
</Step>
<Step>
### Phases I-V

The game has 5 phases, and attacking gets easier as it goes (see the table below). Even after the game has started, you can join until the end of Phase II. You are placed on a team so that the size difference stays within 2 players. If there is no such team, you spectate until a slot opens.
</Step>
<Step>
### Result

When only one team still has a Nexus, that team wins. Phase V is cut off after 20 minutes, and the team with the most Nexus health left at that point wins.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Phases

| Phase | Length | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| I | 10 min | The Nexus cannot be damaged. Use this time to gather resources and gear up |
| II | 10 min | Nexuses can now be damaged. This is the last phase in which new players can join |
| III | 10 min | Diamonds appear in the center. Witches appear |
| IV | 10 min | Bosses appear. Blaze Powder goes on sale at the brewing shop |
| V | up to 20 min | Each hit on a Nexus takes off 2. The phase is cut off after 20 minutes |

## Map (Vanilla)

Official games are played on **Vanilla**, a map built by the Carrot staff. The four bases are in the four corners, and every base is the same layout rotated by 90 degrees.

- A **base** is a round tower. The ground floor has the spawn point, the shop signs, and the Nether portal, and the Nexus is at the very top. Stairs inside the central pillar lead to the upper floors, and a ladder leads to the top.
- Under the **center**, 8 diamond ore blocks appear in Phase III.
- One **witch** appears at the edge of each of the 4 ponds. The color of the banners around a pond is the color of the nearest base's team.
- The **boss gates** are End Portal frames in the caves at the west and east edges. They are open only while a boss is alive.
- Outside the map is the void. You die immediately if you fall into it.

## Nexus

The **End Stone** at each team's base is its Nexus. Any tool can mine it, and each block mined takes 1 health off.

- It cannot be damaged during Phase I. Your own team's Nexus cannot be damaged either.
- Handymen have a chance to heal their own Nexus when they mine it. Healing is the only thing that can raise it above the starting 75, up to 100.
- A team whose Nexus is destroyed can no longer respawn. Surviving players can keep fighting until they die.
- Inside the building that holds a Nexus, blocks cannot be placed or broken (the area is set for each map). The same goes for the areas around spawn points, shops, and furnaces.
- While your Nexus is being damaged, everyone on your team gets a notice and a warning sound. When a teammate damages an enemy Nexus, team chat shows a message like "X damaged Red's Nexus by 1 (69 left)".

## Resources

Items you get from breaking blocks go straight into your inventory. Broken blocks return after a short wait.

| Block | Drops | Respawn time |

| Gold Ore | Raw Gold | 20 sec |

| Emerald Ore | Emerald | 40 sec |
| Redstone Ore | 4-5 Redstone | 20 sec |
| Lapis Lazuli Ore | 4-8 Lapis Lazuli | 10 sec |
| Logs and wood (any tree) | Oak Log | 10 sec |

- Blocks that are mined with a pickaxe, axe, or shovel cannot be broken without that tool. Melons can be broken with an axe or a sword.
- Gravel does not fall.
- Hostile mobs do not spawn (only witches and bosses appear).

## Gear Limits

To keep the gap between long-time players and new players from growing too large, enchantments and potions have strength limits. Only boss rewards can exceed the limits.

| Enchantment | Limit |
|---|---|
| Protection, Projectile Protection, Fire Protection, Blast Protection | II |
| Sharpness, Power | II |
| Efficiency, Unbreaking | III |
| Fire Aspect, Knockback, Punch, Flame | I |
| Thorns | Cannot be applied |

- Enchanting does not require Lapis Lazuli.
- Potions of Strength II, Instant Damage II, and Poison II cannot be made. If you try, you get level I.
- In games where the largest team has 5 players or fewer, Potions of Invisibility cannot be made.
- Enchanted Golden Apples cannot be used.

## Classes

Each class starts with different tools and abilities. Every class starts with a wooden sword, pickaxe, axe, and shovel (or better ones if the class has them), leather armor in your team color, and a compass that points to an enemy Nexus.

### Changing Class

- Enter the **Nether portal at your own base** to open the class screen. When you choose a class, it changes right away (only the class tools are swapped, and the items you have gathered stay with you).
- If you choose from the menu or with `/anni class`, the change takes effect the next time you respawn.

### Unlocking Classes

You can use 5 classes from the start: Civilian, Miner, Lumberjack, Warrior, and Archer. In addition, 3 classes are free to use each week. You can unlock the rest with ANNI points. In custom rooms, all classes are available.

## Witches

- From Phase III, they appear at set places on the map. On Vanilla, that is at the edge of the pond in each team's banner color.
- They stay where they are and cannot be pushed. They attack with potions when you come near.
- They can only be hurt by player attacks (arrows included). When killed, they drop **Glowstone Dust** or **Gunpowder**, and appear again after 5 minutes.

## Bosses

From Phase IV, a boss appears on maps that have a boss location. It is one of Celariel the Ice Golem, Firwen the Fire Golem, or the Wither. On Vanilla, the boss waits in a room in the sky, and you fly there through the End Portal in the cave.

- While a boss is alive, its remaining health is shown in its name, and nearby players see a boss bar.
- When killed, it drops a <Item img="/items/nether_star.png" name="Boss's Blessing" />, and one is given to everyone on the team that picks it up. Right-click it to choose one reward. Rewards are Soulbound and exceed the gear limits.
- To leave, step into a Nether portal in the room and you return to your own spawn.
- A killed boss appears again after 10 minutes. The gates stay closed until then.

## Points and Stats

You earn ANNI points when an official game ends. Points are used to unlock classes. Points for kills, Nexus damage, and bosses also show up in chat the moment you earn them, like "+3 points Kill".

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Kill an enemy | 3 |
| Damage a Nexus once | 1 (2 in Phase V) |
| Destroy a Nexus (the final hit) | 20 |
| Your team kills the boss | 25 (everyone on the team) |
| Win | 100 |
| Remaining health of the winning team's Nexus | 1 per health point |
| Stay in the game until the end | 20 |

Winning and staying to the end are worth the most, so the surest way to earn points is to stay in the game and play to win with your team. You can view stats and rankings from the menu, or with `/anni stats` and `/anni top`. Games in custom rooms do not count toward points or stats.

## During a Game

- **Chat**: Normally only your team can see it. Start a message with `!` to send it to everyone in the game. After the game ends, everyone can see it.
- **Voice chat**: During a game you only hear players on your own team (with the Simple Voice Chat mod, or the web call from `/vc`).
- **Death notices**: If you are killed near a Nexus (within 20 blocks to the side), the message says which one, like "X was killed by Y near Red's Nexus".
- **If you disconnect**: If you return within 90 seconds, you can continue on your original team. The items you were carrying (except Soulbound ones) are dropped on the spot.
- **The void**: If you fall outside or below the map, you die immediately.

- Room games can run at the same time as official games. There is a limit on how many rooms can be open at once, and you cannot create one when it is full.
- In the room settings, you can also turn on "Allow breaking around the Nexus" (off by default). Even when it is on, the areas around spawn points, shops, and furnaces stay protected.
