You can stream and post videos of CarrotCraft without applying. Monetization, such as ad revenue, is fine.

<Callout type="info" title="Request">
We'd appreciate it if you mention in the title or description of your stream or video that you play on CarrotCraft. It is not required.
</Callout>

## Guidelines

By streaming or posting a video, you are considered to have agreed to these guidelines.

### When other players appear

- It is fine for other players to appear during normal play
- Do not keep showing a player who has clearly said they do not want to appear

### Voice chat

CarrotCraft has voice chat, and you can talk to nearby players. Before including another player's voice in a stream or video, get that player's permission once. Everyone involved must agree.

If you are concerned, you can avoid the problem by not installing the voice chat mod or not using the voice chat feature.

### Not allowed

- Calling on viewers to grief or cause trouble
- Calling on viewers to attack or interfere with a specific player
- Calling on viewers to put load on the server

### Stopping a stream

If staff judge that there is a problem, we may ask you individually to stop streaming.

### Requests to remove content

We may ask you to remove content such as:

- Content that gives a false impression of the server or other players by stating things that are not true
- Content made with the aim of putting down a specific player or the server

## Disclaimer

You may show CarrotCraft's in-game chat on stream. However, we take no responsibility for any damage or problems caused by a stream or video.
