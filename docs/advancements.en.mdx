<Callout type="info" title="What are advancements?">
  Goals you complete as you play on the Nation server. There are more than 80, and some are hidden until you complete them.
</Callout>

## 🏆 Viewing advancements

On Java Edition, open the advancements screen with the `L` key. CarrotCraft's advancements are split into five tabs.

| Tab | Main contents |
| --- | --- |
| CarrotCraft | First steps, towns, chatting with others, and more |
| Carrots and Cooking | Carrots, cooking, drinks, farming |
| Money and Trade | Money, shops, auctions, the Compression Artisan |
| Adventure and Combat | Mining, woodcutting, combat, gear |
| Casino | Casino games |

You get a chat message when you complete one.

## 🎖 Titles

Some advancements give you a **title** (32 in total). The title you wear shows as an icon in front of your name in chat and TAB.

The color of a title's frame has a meaning.

| Color | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Red | Only the first player on the server to complete it can have this title |
| Gold | The first player to earn a title that anyone can earn |
| White | A title anyone can have |

Titles you earn are not put on automatically. Choose the one you want to wear.

## 🔧 Wearing a title

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Open `/uc menu`
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Press "Titles" in the bottom row
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Press the title you want (press "Remove" to take it off)
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also use commands.

| Command | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `/ctitle list` | List the titles you have |
| `/ctitle set <ID>` | Wear a title |
| `/ctitle clear` | Remove your title |

<Callout type="warn" title="A title or a badge, not both">
  Only one of a title or a [Compression Artisan badge](/en/docs/artisan) can show in front of your name. On the Nation server, wearing a title removes your badge, and wearing a badge removes your title. On the Resource server's TAB, your title shows instead of your badge while you wear one.
</Callout>

## 🎨 Seeing title icons

Title icons are in the resource pack. Accept the resource pack when the server asks you on joining. Once accepted, it does not reload when you move between servers.

## ❓ FAQ

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="My title shows as a square">
    Without the resource pack, title icons show as squares. Rejoin the server and accept the resource pack.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My title came off">
    Wearing a Compression Artisan badge removes your title. You can wear only one of a title or a badge.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The tabs do not show on the advancements screen">
    Rejoin the server and they will show.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
