**Every two weeks**, nations are ranked using various indicators.
The top 2 nations get rewards for the next two weeks.

## 🏆 How is the ranking decided?

Your nation moves up when its residents **play normally and the nation is lively**.
There's no specific task you need to repeat.

<Callout type="warn" title="The criteria are not public">
  What we look at, the scores and the ranking in progress are not published.
</Callout>

## 🎁 Rewards for the top 2

### Higher dividend yield

| Rank | Weekly yield | Over two weeks |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| 🥇 1st | **5%** | 2 payouts (every Saturday) |
| 🥈 2nd | **3%** | 2 payouts (every Saturday) |

- The extra is **paid by the staff**, so the treasury doesn't go down
- If the king's own yield is higher, that yield is used as is
- As with normal dividends, the king's shares get nothing
- Dividends are calculated the usual way (see "Dividends" on [Nation stocks](/docs/kabu))

### Easier share issues

The yearly share issue limit goes from **30% to 60%** of total shares.
Shareholders still vote as usual.

### 👑 for the 1st place king

The king of the 1st place nation gets 👑 next to their name. It moves when 1st place changes at the next ranking.

<Callout type="info">
  The dividend and share issue rewards are **only for listed nations**. If an unlisted nation is 1st, it only gets the 👑.
</Callout>

## 📅 The two weeks

<Steps>
<Step>
### Sunday 22:00 JST — close

The two weeks end and the ranking is decided.
</Step>
<Step>
### The next two weeks — rewards

The top 2 nations' rewards start. Dividends are paid twice, every Saturday at 12:00 JST.
</Step>
<Step>
### Sunday 22:00 JST again — next close

The rewards and the 👑 move to the next ranking's winners.
</Step>
</Steps>
