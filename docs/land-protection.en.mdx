<Callout type="info" title="How to read this page">
**Land Protection (CarrotGuard)** lets you protect your land outside towns **by area**. Everything — choosing the size, creating, renewing and managing — can be done from the `/guard` menu. This is separate from [chest protection](/docs/bolt) (single containers) and [Towny](/docs/towny) (whole towns).
</Callout>

## 🛡 What is Land Protection?

It protects an **area you choose**, preventing other players from breaking, placing, or stealing inside it. There are two types.

<Cards>
<Card title="Personal Protection" description="Your own protection, available to everyone. Must be renewed (free) every 7 days. Creating it is free during your beginner period." />
<Card title="Town Protection" description="Created by mayors and vice mayors to protect land outside the town. No expiry. Supports members and detailed settings." />
</Cards>

|  | Personal | Town |
|---|---|---|
| Who can create | Everyone | Mayors and vice mayors |
| Count / size | Up to 3 per player, each up to **64×64** | Town total up to **residents ÷ 2 × 512 blocks²** (at least **64×64** is always allowed) |
| Cost | **Free** during the beginner period (14 days after joining); after that, **paid once on creation** (exactly **10,000 CC** for 64×64, about 2.44 CC per block²) | About 2.44 CC per block² (max **10,000 CC**) |
| Expiry | **Must be renewed every 7 days** (renewal is free) | None |
| Members / settings | Not available (fixed protection) | Available |

<Callout type="warn" title="Do not protect others' builds without permission">
Enclosing another player's build or land within your own protection **without their permission is prohibited**. If discovered, the protection will be **forcibly removed** and the offender **subject to punishment** (Rules 3.1).
</Callout>

## ✏️ How to create

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open `/guard` and choose "Choose a size and create"

**Stand in the middle** of the area you want to protect, open `/guard`, and press **"Choose a size and create"** in the bottom row.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose the size

Use the **16×16 / 32×32 / 48×48 / 64×64** buttons or the width/depth **+/- (by 1 or 8)** buttons (the full height is always included).
**"Go outside and check the outline"** closes the menu and shows the selected area with particles (open `/guard` again to continue).
</Step>
<Step>
### Create

Press **"Go to the create screen with this area"**, check the **type, size, and cost**, then press **">> Create"**. If you can't create it, the button turns gray and shows the reason.

<Callout type="info" title="You can also use a wooden hoe">
To choose any shape, hold a wooden hoe, **left-click for point 1 and right-click for point 2**, then open `/guard` to create with that area.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Mayors and vice mayors can switch the type">
Mayors and vice mayors can use the middle button on the confirmation screen to switch between **personal ⇔ town** protection.
</Callout>
</Step>
</Steps>

## ⏰ Renewing personal protection (every 7 days)

Personal protection is **removed automatically unless renewed every 7 days** (your builds stay). Renewal is **free** and can be done any time.

- `/guard renew` … renews **all** of your personal protections
- `/guard` → **"Renew all personal protections"** (clock) in the bottom row
- `/guard` → click a protection → **"Renew"** (clock)

Renewing sets the new deadline to **7 days from the moment you renew** (days do not stack). From 2 days before the deadline you'll get reminders on login and while playing. If a protection expires while you're away, you'll be told the next time you log in.

## 🛡 What is protected

| | Personal | Town (when created) |
|---|---|---|
| Breaking / placing blocks | Blocked for non-members | Blocked for non-members |
| Opening chests / using doors etc. | Blocked for non-members | Blocked for non-members |
| Attacking animals | Blocked for non-members | Blocked for non-members |
| Fire spread | Stopped | Stopped |
| Explosions (TNT, creepers, etc.) | Stopped (the owner's TNT won't explode either) | Stopped |
| PvP / monster spawning | Server default | Server default |

Personal protection **cannot be changed**. Town protection can be changed in the settings below.

## ⚙️ Town protection settings (toggles)

`/guard` → click a town protection → **Settings** lets you switch the following **ᴏɴ (stop) / ᴏꜰꜰ (allow)** inside that protection.

| Toggle | Applies to | When ᴏɴ |
|---|---|---|
| Block breaking | Non-members | Can't break blocks |
| Block placing | Non-members | Can't place blocks |
| Use / open chests | Non-members | Can't use / open |
| Attacking animals | Non-members | Can't attack animals |
| PvP | Whole area (members too) | No PvP |
| Monster spawning | Whole area | Monsters won't spawn |
| Fire spread | Whole area | Fire won't spread |
| Explosions (TNT, creepers, etc.) | Whole area | Explosions won't break blocks |

## 👥 Adding members (town protection only)

From a town protection's management screen → **Add member**, you can add people who may build and use chests inside it.

- **Online players**: click their head from the list
- **Offline players**: use **"Enter a name (anvil)"** to type a name (players who haven't joined yet can also be specified)

Any **mayor or vice mayor of the town** can manage its town protections (not only the creator — it keeps working after the mayor changes).

<Callout type="info" title="Personal protection has no members">
Personal protection is for one player. To build together with friends, create a town and use **town protection**.
</Callout>

## 🚫 Where you cannot create protection

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Common (both types)">
- Overlapping an existing protection or a **Towny town plot**
- Where a **town is within 150 blocks of the protection's edge**
- On another player's build/land (unauthorized protection breaks the rules)
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Personal protection">
- Exceeding the **max count (3)**
- Exceeding the **max size (64×64)**
- After the beginner period, if you **don't have enough money (CC)**
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Town protection">
- You are **not a mayor or vice mayor**
- The town's total protected area exceeds the **cap (residents ÷ 2 × 512 blocks², at least 64×64)**
- You **don't have enough money (CC)**
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

<Callout type="info" title="Towns can't claim over other players' protection">
When a town expands, it **cannot claim plots overlapping someone else's personal protection or another town's town protection**. It can only claim over its own residents' personal protections and its own town protections (the protection is then removed).
</Callout>

---

## 🗑 When protection is removed

- **Personal protection expired**: it wasn't renewed within 7 days
- **Overlapping a town plot**: a protection overlapped by your own town's new plot is auto-removed
- **Manual deletion**: management screen → Delete → confirm on the confirmation screen
- **Rule violations**: protections confirmed as violations (e.g. protecting others' builds) are forcibly removed by staff

In every case **your builds stay** — only the protection is removed.

<Callout type="info" title="Griefing can be recovered">
Even if a protection is lost and the area is griefed, staff **may be able to restore it via rollback** if you report it ([Compensation & Rollback](/docs/rules#6-補填ロールバック)).
</Callout>

## 📋 Commands

| Command | Description |
|---|---|
| `/guard` | Protection menu (list / create / renew / manage / show borders — everything works from the menu) |
| `/guard renew` | Renew all your personal protections (free) |
| `/guard tool` | How to select an area (wooden hoe) |
| `/guard create` | Create from your selection (without opening the GUI) |
| `/guard list` | List your own protections (with days left for personal ones) |
| `/guard border` | Show nearby protection borders with particles (run again to hide) |

Admins have `/guardadmin` (a menu listing every protection, teleport, renew, settings, delete, and more). All actions are logged.
